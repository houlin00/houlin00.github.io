Ph.D. Candidate @ UC Santa Cruz

Hi, I'm Richard Wang.

I am a computational linguist and speech scientist specializing in ASR evaluation, speech corpus analysis, and acoustic modeling. My research focuses on the speech variation that standard systems routinely ignore — accents, dialects, and casual speech — across both low-resource and widely spoken languages. My goal is to make speech-to-text products more accurate, robust, and natural for the full range of real-world users.

ASR Evaluation on Severely Under-Resourced Languages

Conducted independent evaluation of Meta's Omnilingual ASR model across five severely under-resourced Munda languages, testing both training languages and zero-shot generalization. Identified systematic failure modes including decoder looping, script divergence, and mislabeled training data. Governed and annotated 90+ hours of speech corpus data, standardizing QA protocols across a distributed team.

ASR Evaluation Low-Resource Languages Model Benchmarking
UC Santa Cruz 2022 – Present

Acoustic Modeling of Overlooked Speech Variation

Built end-to-end acoustic analysis pipelines in Python and R for feature extraction, signal processing, and automated quality control, enabling analysis at 10x the scale of manual methods. Applied mixed-effects regression, bootstrapping, and ML classification across 20,000+ model iterations over 8 interacting variables to model speech variation, overturning the prevailing theoretical account in the field.
