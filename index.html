Ph.D. Candidate @ UC Santa Cruz

Hi, I'm Richard Wang.

I am a computational linguist and speech scientist specializing in ASR evaluation, speech corpus analysis, and acoustic modeling. My research focuses on the speech variation that standard systems routinely ignore — accents, dialects, and casual speech — across both low-resource and widely spoken languages. My goal is to make speech-to-text products more accurate and natural for the full range of real-world users.

Featured Research

Selected work in ASR evaluation, speech variation modeling, and computational corpus analysis.

Living Tongues Institute Summer 2026

ASR Evaluation on Severely Under-Resourced Languages

Independent evaluation of Meta's Omnilingual ASR model across five severely under-resourced Munda languages, testing both training languages and zero-shot generalization. Identified systematic failure modes including decoder looping, script divergence, and mislabeled training data.

ASR Evaluation Low-Resource Languages Model Benchmarking
UC Santa Cruz 2022 – Present

Acoustic Modeling of Overlooked Speech Variation

Built end-to-end acoustic analysis pipelines in Python and R for feature extraction, signal processing, and automated quality control. Applied mixed-effects regression, bootstrapping, and ML classification across 20,000+ model iterations; findings overturned the prevailing theoretical account in the field.
